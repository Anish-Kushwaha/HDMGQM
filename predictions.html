Primary observable sectors

Polarized photon transport

Finite-field QED response can alter polarization-mode propagation. The test is not “birefringence exists” — that is established QED — but whether the self-consistent HMGQM interior, surface and magnetosphere produce a residual relative to a baseline model.

Gravitational-wave modes

Axisymmetric magnetic structure can shift or split mode spectra. The numerical shift is only a prediction after the coupled perturbation operator and boundary conditions are solved.

Structure & deformation

The equilibrium family can provide M(R), Λ(M), I(M), magnetic energy, magnetic ellipticity Q(M), and electromagnetic/light-curve quantities.

How a prediction becomes evidence

Baseline

Match external observables and propagate nuisance parameters in a conventional magnetized-star model.

Residual

Compute HMGQM − baseline differences and ask whether they survive uncertainty volume and model freedom.

Replication

Prefer correlated signatures produced by the same interior solution across independent messenger channels.

The scientifically interesting quantity is often the residual between two self-consistent models.

What would count as strong evidence?

A robustly converged new equilibrium branch, a non-analytic thermodynamic feature correlated with the H-regime, a stable range of configurations with significant anisotropic stress, and an observable difference that remains after nuisance-parameter marginalization would materially strengthen the framework.
