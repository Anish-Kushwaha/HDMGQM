Hyper-Dense Magneto-Gravitational Quantum Matter

A research framework for ultra-magnetized, ultra-dense compact matter that couples classical general relativity, finite-field strong-field QED, Landau-quantized microphysics, electromagnetic stress and anisotropic principal stresses in stationary axisymmetric equilibrium.

Scientific position

The Volume 3 document describes H-Matter as a regime hypothesis. A stronger claim of a distinct thermodynamic phase requires a non-analytic thermodynamic feature or a well-defined order parameter, together with reproducible calculations and observational discrimination.

What the framework asks

Central hypothesis

In a sufficiently dense, strongly magnetized compact object, Landau quantization of charged particles, QED vacuum response, electromagnetic stress and relativistic gravity can all become dynamically important together. The resulting stress-energy is anisotropic and global equilibrium is generically axisymmetric.

The central scientific question is whether this coupled system creates a quantitatively new regime, a stable equilibrium branch, or an observable signature that existing magnetized compact-star models cannot reproduce.

Operating picture

Local thermodynamics → magnetization and principal stresses → total covariant stress tensor → Einstein-Maxwell-matter equilibrium → stability → transport and observables.
